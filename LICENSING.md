## Commercial licence

The AGPL suits most people. An organisation whose policy does not allow AGPL software in its products, or that wants to run a changed FlatBB without publishing the changes, can obtain a **commercial licence** for a fee. It grants the same code under terms that do not require sharing modifications. Ask on https://www.flatbb.com (a private message to the administrator) for the terms.
